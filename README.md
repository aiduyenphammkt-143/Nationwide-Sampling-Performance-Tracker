# Sales Sampling Performance Dashboard
Built a scalable performance-tracking solution that helped ensure consistent execution of a nationwide product sampling campaign, supported weekly executive decision-making, and enabled proactive intervention for underperforming sales teams.

---

## Overview

This project was developed during the nationwide launch campaign of **Nabati Strawberry Cheesecake Wafer** at **Nabati Vietnam**.

As the campaign expanded across multiple sales teams and regions, stakeholders needed a reliable way to monitor execution progress, identify underperforming areas, and ensure timely intervention.

To address this challenge, I led the project and developed a Power BI dashboard that transformed daily sampling reports into actionable insights for both field teams and executive management.

---

## Business Challenge

The campaign was executed at scale:

- 100+ Distributor Sales Teams
- 13 Areas
- 3 Regions
- More than 1 year of nationwide implementation (separated into 2 phases)

Without a centralized monitoring system, management faced difficulties in:

- Tracking project progress consistently
- Identifying slow-moving regions and distributors
- Following up on execution issues in a timely manner
- Reporting nationwide performance to executives

---

## ⚡ Solution

Developed a Power BI dashboard as a single source of truth for project monitoring.

### Key Capabilities

* Track nationwide sampling progress
* Monitor KPI achievement by Region → Area → Distributor
* Drill down to the responsible Sales Supervisor (execution owner)
* Identify underperforming teams early
* Support weekly executive reporting
* Enable data-driven follow-up and intervention

### Business Impacts

* Improved execution discipline across 100+ sales teams nationwide
* Created end-to-end visibility from Region Manager to Sales Supervisor
* Enabled fact-based follow-up and faster intervention for underperforming teams
* Helped ensure the new product reached consumers through consistent sampling execution

## Project Workflow

```text
Marketing Launch sampling program & KPI
    ↓
Sales Teams

Report results after each event

Power BI Dashboard
    ↓
Track progress & performance

Marketing
    ↓
Follow up delayed teams
Support issue resolution
Report project status to executives
```
